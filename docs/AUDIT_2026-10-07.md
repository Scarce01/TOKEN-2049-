## 10. Tracing on chain (same day, after section 9)

The only path that did not exist: Trek proposes, nothing verified or wrote it on chain. Built and tested on the
simulation chain (local fork, CRE CLI simulate --broadcast):

- `workflows/patrol` trigger 5 `verify-edge` (HTTP): reads each proposed transfer (receipt, settled at the anchor),
  checks token/from/to/amount, requires the parent to be a listed suspect with valid evidence (or verified earlier in
  the same batch), a per-token minimum amount, then writes derived THREATs. Evidence hash and expiry (24 h) are computed
  by the workflow. Logic in `src/logic/edges.ts`, 9 unit tests.
- `analysis/trace_bybit/fork_source.py`: Trek on any JSON-RPC with the real chain id (trek.py untouched), 1 test.
- `services/redteam/demo/trace-fork.ts` (`pnpm demo:trace`): **11/11** on the fork. Decoy probe to freeze 15 s; last
  laundering hop to derived THREAT 14 s (both include about 14 s CLI compile). Negatives: forged amount and a real
  transfer from a non-suspect parent are refused; a replay does not count twice; a payout to the traced address is
  PENDING 42 and refused.
- Prevention E2E 8/8 (new `E2E_ORG=b`); case A also checks network follow (any active confirmed threat delays every
  member by L1, the keeper pays after the delay).
- `verify:design`: 47 pass (new D113), 1 fail (D28), 34 not-yet. The not-yet items need acceptance scene scripts
  (docs/32, 33, 36, 37) that were never written; their unit tests pass.
