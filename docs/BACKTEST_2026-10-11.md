## 4. Decoy generator [synthetic]

`run_epoch` six epochs in a row, one decoy burned per epoch (attacker touches it), 1,000 synthetic accounts, four seeds; plus the 100-account demo set. Nothing written to `secrets/`.

| Data | Gate pass | AUC upper range | Honeyword rate | Online decoys | Time per epoch |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| seed 2049 | 6 / 6 | 0.524 to 0.596 | 0.13 to 0.22 | 6 to 8 | 9 to 11 s |
| seed 7 | 6 / 6 | 0.531 to 0.591 | 0.16 to 0.24 | 6 to 8 | 8 to 9 s |
| seed 11 | 6 / 6 | 0.531 to 0.602 | 0.15 to 0.22 | 6 to 8 | 9 s |
| seed 1234 | 4 / 6 | 0.499 to 0.564 | 0.15 to 0.21 | **0** in epoch 1, 7 in epoch 5 | 8 to 9 s |
| demo 100 accounts | 0 / 2 | 0.598, 0.635 | 0.17, 0.21 | 0 | 4 to 5 s |

Gate is two-sided: AUC upper at most 0.65 and AUC lower at least 0.35 (`model.py:279`). Both seed-1234 failures and the demo set fail on the **lower** bound (0.328, 0.348; demo 0.267, 0.233), meaning the folds are too noisy, not that the decoys are easy to spot.

- **Coverage gap:** when the first epoch fails there is nothing to keep, so the exchange runs with zero decoys for a whole epoch. Later failures keep the previous set (7 online). Needs a fallback (retry with a new draw, or keep the static seed set) before this goes live.
- Old method still fails as expected: `decoy_auc.py --top 60` AUC 0.706, CI [0.51, 0.88].
- Attacker side (`adversary_sim.py`, 5,000 runs, 10 decoys in 200 accounts, 20 probes): random 66.1 % hit (formula 66.0 %), whale-first 99.3 %, careful 99.1 %; mean 5.3 to 8.8 probes before the first hit. Placing decoys in the top-50 balance band is what makes the greedy attacker trip.
